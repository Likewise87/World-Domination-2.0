# Getting started

Use this page to prepare the mod list, create the world, and identify the controls needed for a first campaign.

## Requirements and load order

World Domination 2.0 requires:

- RimWorld **1.6**
- **Harmony**
- **Vanilla Expanded Framework** (Vanilla Factions Expanded Core / VEF)

Load **Harmony** and **Vanilla Expanded Framework** before **World Domination 2.0**. WD must load after both required frameworks.

Do not enable either incompatible mod:

- The original World Domination, packageId `TSA.WorldDomination`
- Visit Settlements, packageId `alt4s.visitsettlements`

The old World Domination package overlaps the successor's systems. Visit Settlements conflicts with WD settlement visiting and map generation.

## First session checklist

1. **Review WD settings before world creation.** Defaults are intended to provide a complete campaign, but WD is highly configurable.
2. **Generate the world and open WD: World Setup.** Use the WD icon during world setup to review or recreate settlements before choosing your colony tile.
3. **Check settlement tiers.** Toggle settlement tier labels on the world map and confirm that the generated distribution suits the campaign.
4. **Choose a colony site with the strategic map in mind.** Nearby hostile settlements can become threats. Friendly settlements can provide diplomacy and support opportunities.
5. **Open the WD tab after landing.** The dashboard summarizes threats, outposts, travelers, world statistics, and other WD windows.
6. **Inspect the world-map WD menu.** This menu controls overlays, labels, relationship highlights, outpost placement previews, and access to major WD windows.
7. **Select nearby settlements and tiles.** Selection gizmos expose actions that apply to the chosen settlement, outpost, traveler, or world tile.
8. **Watch the first travelers.** NPC travelers are abstract strength pools, not groups of generated pawns. Select one to read its faction, mission, destination, and current strength.
9. **Plan the first outpost.** Use the establish-outpost simulation from the WD world-map menu to preview tile requirements and material costs before sending a real pawn Caravan.
10. **Review raid behavior.** Storyteller raids from WD-managed factions are blocked by default. Their attacks arrive through visible WD raid travelers.
