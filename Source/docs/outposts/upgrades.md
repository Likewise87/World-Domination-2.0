# Outpost upgrades

Upgrades are permanent improvements built from the **Upgrades** tab. Universal upgrades are available to every outpost. Type-locked upgrades appear only on their matching specialization.

Material costs and research requirements can be enabled or disabled in mod settings. When enabled, the outpost must have access to every listed material and the required research must be complete.

## Planning upgrades

Defensive strength is useful at every exposed outpost, but specialization upgrades usually provide the best return at already productive sites. Improve strong fertility, animal, fish, and mining tiles before trying to rescue poor ones. Hospitals belong at sites that frequently spend offensive strength. Tunnels are most useful inside a cluster of mutually supporting sites. Silos and hydroponics stabilize isolated garrisons.

??? note "Advanced"
    Bonuses shown in the tables are the definition values before mod-setting multipliers or other systems. Percentage-point tile bonuses add to the relevant world-tile score. Production and recovery percentages act through their respective outpost calculations.

    Upgrades sharing an upgrade-line ID represent tiers of the same installation. Independent lines can stack even when they are in the same category. The Upgrades tab shows the effective result and whether a previous tier is replaced.
