# Outpost types

Outpost tiers describe the specialization's strategic value and founding difficulty. The establishment window shows the exact occupants, cumulative skills, research, materials, and tile conditions required at the selected location.

## Tier 1

### Farming Outpost

Produces crop goods such as food and cloth. Output scales with the world's tile fertility, and irrigation upgrades can improve the site's effective fertility. Place it on a tile with a long growing season, strong plant density, and favorable terrain.

### Hunting Outpost

Produces meat, leather, and wool. Output scales with animal abundance on the world tile and the occupants' Animals skill. Hunting upgrades improve local animal abundance and defense.

### Fishing Outpost

Produces one selected fish species. Output scales with fish stocks on an ocean-coast land tile and the occupants' Animals skill. Fishing upgrades increase fish abundance and defense. **Odyssey is required.**

### Scavenging Outpost

Produces a random assortment of goods on a fixed cycle. Each colonist contributes **500 silver of market value per delivery**. At least **2 colonists** are required to found it.

### Mining Outpost

Produces stone blocks, ores, and components. Output scales with the tile's mining efficiency, which is based chiefly on hilliness and supported tile modifiers. Mining lift upgrades improve mining efficiency and defense.

### Ranch Outpost

Produces milk, eggs, and wool. Output scales with tile fertility and the occupants' Animals skill. Barn upgrades improve production efficiency and defense.

### Research Outpost

Applies continuous progress to the colony's active research project instead of delivering goods. Output scales with Intellectual skill. Microscopes and advanced lab equipment improve research efficiency.

### Power Plant Outpost

Provides remote power directly to the colony without local wiring or fuel deliveries. It does not produce goods. Power upgrades add further remote generation.

### Warehouse Outpost

Stores items without spoilage. Other outposts can route cycle deliveries into it, and stored stock can be shipped manually or automatically to a colony or another warehouse. Its productivity aura benefits eligible nearby producers.

## Tier 2

### Recruiting Outpost

Recruits new colonists each cycle. Recruitment scales with cumulative Social skill and with up to the top three nearby neutral or allied settlement partners from each faction. Nearby partners also influence which faction xenotypes can appear among recruits.

### Trading Outpost

Produces silver each cycle. Output scales with cumulative Social skill and up to the top three nearby neutral or allied partners per faction. It must be founded within range of suitable settlements.

### Biofuel Outpost

Produces chemfuel and scales with Crafting skill. It requires **Biofuel Refining** research. With Combat Extended, sufficiently skilled occupants can also unlock supported fuel products.

### Rapid Response Outpost

Dispatches fast virtual counter-travelers against configured hostile travelers in range. Hostile travelers crossing its tile trigger an immediate attack. After **Transport Pods** research, it can also launch selected colonists by drop pod. It does not produce goods.

### Academy Outpost

Trains occupants in one selected RimWorld skill per cycle. The teacher's skill determines training output and the student level cap. It does not deliver goods.

### Drug Lab Outpost

Produces smokeleaf, psychite drugs, go-juice, and wake-up according to occupant skill and completed drug research. Output scales with Intellectual skill. Founding requires **Drug Production**.

## Tier 3

### Embassy Outpost

Improves goodwill with nearby factions each cycle. Neutral and allied factions qualify by default, while temporarily hostile factions can qualify when settings permit. It uses the highest settlement tier per faction and scales with Social skill. Permanently hostile factions are excluded.

### Fabrication Outpost

Produces industrial components and, with the appropriate skill and research, advanced components. Output scales with Crafting skill. Founding requires **Fabrication** research.

### Artillery Outpost

Also called the Mortar Outpost in some settings and internal labels. It fires long-range mortar strikes at hostile targets and uses the garrison's Shooting skill. Upgrades unlock anti-air fire and improve accuracy, cooldown, shell damage, and defense. It does not produce goods.

## Choosing a type

Use a production outpost where its governing tile score is high, not merely where the tile is convenient. Use warehouses, power plants, research sites, and academies where their coverage and security matter more than terrain. Place recruiting, trading, and embassy sites only after checking the People Radius overlay and the partner list in the establishment UI.

??? note "Advanced"
    Recruiting partners contribute neighbor points according to settlement tier:

    | Partner tier | Neighbor points |
    |---|---:|
    | Tier 1 | 1 |
    | Tier 2 | 2 |
    | Tier 3 | 3.5 |
    | Tier 4 | 5 |

    Only the top three partners per faction count, ordered by tier and then distance. Add all qualifying points together. Every **3 neighbor points** grants **1 additional recruit** for that cycle beyond the Social-based result.

    Selecting a specific skill-training priority produces **30% fewer recruits** that cycle, but gives those recruits a minimum level in the chosen skill. Selecting **Any** keeps the full recruit count and assigns random skills.
