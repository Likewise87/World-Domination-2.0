WORLD DOMINATION 2.0

Caravans

Joint Caravans

Road Builder Caravan

Builds roads on the world map. Used by NPC factions and by player outpost road missions.

  • Faction crews expand the road network
  • Player outposts can dispatch the same crew type
  • Construction progress is visible on the map

Trader Caravan

Trade and diplomacy traffic on the world map. NPCs run traders; player settlement actions use the same look.

  • NPC traders move between destinations
  • Player purchase, gift, bribe, and negotiate caravans share this icon
  • Can be intercepted in caravan clashes

Builder Crew

Construction crews for world-tile fortifications. Player outposts send them to build or clear road blocks and spike traps.

  • Road Block Builder places or clears road blocks
  • Trap Builder lays or clears spike traps
  • Blocks slow traffic; traps damage hostile passers

Mortar Shell

World-map ballistic shell fired from an Artillery Outpost at a hostile target.

  • Very fast
  • Ignores terrain
  • Can be shot down by anti-air guns

Flak Shell

Anti-air flak shell fired at hostile drop pods and shells in range.

  • Same ballistic hop rules as mortar shells
  • Requires the Anti-Air Gun upgrade
  • Separate short cooldown from mortar strikes

Decontamination Crew

Outpost crew scrubbing pollution from world tiles after the upgrade unlock.

  • Requires Decontamination Equipment upgrade
  • Requires Biotech
  • Works a target tile after arriving in transit

Player Caravans

Outpost Raider Caravan

Player-directed raid launched from an outpost against a chosen target.

  • Uses the outpost offensive strength
  • Can clash with other travelers on the way

Outpost Delivery Caravan

Moves produced goods from outposts to colonies or warehouses.

  • Carries outpost delivery shipments
  • Can route through warehouses

Outpost Upgrade Caravan

Delivers materials for an outpost upgrade order.

  • Spawned when an upgrade is purchased
  • Applies the upgrade when it arrives

Rapid Response Caravan

Interception caravan sent from a Rapid Response outpost against enemy targets.

  • Uses garrison combat strength
  • Scales with Shooting and Melee skill
  • Clashes with hostile travelers on contact

Rapid Response Drop Pods

Ballistic Rapid Response dispatch that drops colonists onto a target after Transport Pods research.

  • Ignores terrain like other drop-pod flights
  • Delivers selected pawns to the target
  • Requires Transport Pods research

Diplomacy Caravans

Settlement Purchase

Diplomacy caravan sent to buy a settlement with silver.

  • Costs silver based on settlement value
  • Transfers ownership on successful arrival

Settlement Gift

Caravan delivering a gift to improve relations with a settlement faction.

  • Spends goods or silver as a gift
  • Improves goodwill on arrival

Settlement Bribe

Caravan carrying a bribe to sway a settlement toward your interests.

  • Higher cost than a simple gift
  • Stronger diplomatic payoff if it arrives

Diplomacy Negotiate

Negotiation caravan sent to talk terms with a target settlement.

  • Resolves the chosen diplomacy action on arrival
  • Can be intercepted like other travelers

NPC Caravans

Raider Caravan

Hostile force traveling across the world map to attack a settlement, outpost, or colony.

  • Strength-based combat on arrival
  • Can clash with player or Rapid Response caravans
  • Takes attrition while traveling

Raider Drop Pods

Tier 4 ballistic raid caravan that follows an as the crow flies path.

  • Ignores terrain movement costs
  • Can be shot down by anti-air guns
  • Much faster than ground raiders

Expansion Caravan

Faction settlers traveling to found a new Tier 1 settlement.

  • Founds a new settlement on arrival
  • Cancels if the tile is blocked or saturated
